MEMENTO MORI

THE MAN BEHIND THE CROWN

A Life of Wisdom.

Marcus Aurelius was born on April 26, 121 AD, in Rome. He grew up in a prominent Roman family and developed an early interest in learning, literature and philosophy. Unlike many people of his position, he was deeply attracted to simple living and philosophical reflection.

In 161 AD, Marcus became Emperor of Rome. His reign was filled with difficult challenges, including military conflicts, political instability and a devastating plague. Despite his responsibilities, he continued to study philosophy and reflect on his actions.

Marcus was strongly influenced by Stoicism, a philosophy that teaches people to focus on what they can control, develop good character and accept what lies beyond their control. He believed that wisdom, justice, courage and self-discipline were essential qualities.

His personal reflections were eventually preserved in a book known as Meditations. Written primarily for his own guidance, these thoughts continue to inspire readers around the world. His legacy is not simply that of an emperor, but of a man who constantly tried to improve himself.
